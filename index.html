How this portal works

Each study day contains four checkboxes: Quant, Verbal, Vocabulary, and Review. Mark a block complete after doing the work. Progress and notes are automatically synchronized to your private Supabase account. Sign into the same account on another device to continue your studies. Internet access is required for saving.

The daily tasks follow your personalized 12-week study plan. Course naming or video ordering can change on GregMat and PrepSwift, so use the listed topic to find the closest matching lesson.
